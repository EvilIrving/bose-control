# Bose Connector

Control Bose headphones over Bluetooth. No account, no cloud. Settings are written to the headset and stay on this device.

Adjust noise cancelling, transparency, and custom modes, equalizer, immersive audio, and wind block, plus multipoint and auto-answer, without an account or a cloud service.
